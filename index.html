Valorant stats for your community, right inside Root: each member's rank and recent matches, a leaderboard for the community, and Riot's official top-players ladder.

How it works in Root

1

Members opt in

A member links their Riot account with Riot's own sign-in (Riot Sign On). The app never sees their password, and only members who link are ever shown.

2

Their stats appear

Rank, the last ten matches (agent, map, score, K/D/A, ACS, headshot rate) and averages over the last twenty, from Riot's official API.

3

The community competes

A leaderboard of linked members by rank, K/D, win rate, ACS or headshots, plus Riot's official ranked ladder for every region.

4

Members stay in control

Pressing Unlink deletes everything the app stored about that member, straight away.

Built to Riot's developer policies

  • Only Riot's official API, with a registered product key
  • Players opt in through Riot Sign On before their stats are shown
  • Unlinking deletes the player's data
  • No MMR or Elo estimates
  • No scouting of opponents before or during matches
  • No live in-game overlays or helpers
  • No betting or gambling of any kind
  • No Riot logos; the legal notice is shown in the app

Status

Valorant Tracker is in development. The app is built and tested with a Riot development key: Riot ID lookup, regions, game content and the ranked ladder all work with live data.

Match history needs Riot's production key, which we're applying for now. Riot sign-in will replace the testing sign-in at the same time.
