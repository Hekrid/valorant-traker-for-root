Valorant Tracker privacy policy

Valorant Tracker is an app for Root communities that shows Valorant stats for members who choose to link their Riot account.

What we store

Only for members who link their Riot account, and only inside the community where they linked it:

  • your Root user id in that community,
  • your Riot account's player id (PUUID), Riot ID (name and tag) and region,
  • your current rank and a summary of your last 20 matches (map, agent, result, kills, deaths, assists, score and headshot rate), and averages worked out from them.

We don't store your Riot password; Riot sign-in happens on Riot's own page and we only receive your account's public id and name.

Who can see it

Linking your account makes your Valorant stats visible to every member of that community. Members who haven't linked are never shown.

The "Top players" tab shows Riot's official public ranked leaderboard as Riot publishes it.

Where data comes from and goes

Stats come from Riot Games' official API. Requests go through the Valorant Tracker gateway, which caches Riot's answers for a short time and doesn't keep anything else. We don't sell or share data and there are no ads or trackers.

Removing your data

Press Unlink in the app. Your link and stored matches are deleted from that community straight away. If the app is removed from a community, its data goes with it.
