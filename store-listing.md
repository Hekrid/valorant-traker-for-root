# Valorant Tracker store listing

## Name

Valorant Tracker

## Short description

Your community's Valorant stats: link your Riot account, see your rank and matches, and climb your community's leaderboard.

## Description

Valorant Tracker brings Valorant stats into your Root community.

- **My stats:** your rank, your recent matches (agent, map, score, K/D/A, ACS and headshot rate) and your averages over the last 20 games.
- **Community leaderboard:** see who's on top in your community, by rank, K/D, win rate, ACS or headshots. Tap anyone to see their matches.
- **Top players:** Riot's official ranked ladder for every region, updated through the act.

Members choose to join: linking your Riot account is your opt-in, and unlinking deletes your data straight away. Members who haven't linked never appear.

No ads, no gambling.

## Privacy summary

Stores, only for members who link: Root user id, Riot player id, Riot ID, region, rank and a summary of the last 20 matches. Visible to the community's members. Unlink deletes it. Full policy: `../privacy.md`.

## For Riot's production key application

Valorant Tracker is an app for communities on Root (a community chat platform). Members link their Riot account with Riot sign-in (RSO) to opt in; the app then shows their rank, recent matches and averages to their own community, and a community leaderboard of linked members. It also shows the official ranked leaderboard. Free for players, with no MMR estimates, scouting, live in-game features or monetization. APIs used: ACCOUNT-V1, VAL-MATCH-V1, VAL-RANKED-V1, VAL-CONTENT-V1, RSO.
